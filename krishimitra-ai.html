How the recommendation engine works

Three stages turn a static list of schemes into a ranked, personalised plan.

STAGE 1

Rule-based filtering

State, crop, land size, income, category, gender and age conditions are checked against every scheme.

STAGE 2

TF‑IDF + cosine ranking

Your profile and interests are vectorised and compared against each scheme's description to score relevance.

STAGE 3

Decision support

Readiness scores, missing documents and a day-by-day action plan are generated for your top matches.
